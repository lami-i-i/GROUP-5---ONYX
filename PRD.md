**2\. Problem Statement**

I have noticed that Babcock students face many difficulties when it comes to getting academic materials.   
Sometimes many students depend on their senior students to get past questions, while others rely solely on shared Google Drive folders or search for materials themselves.   
These methods are not always reliable for students and they are very stressful and cause a lot of inconsistencies and inefficiencies when it comes to studying.   
Sometimes materials may also be incomplete. They may be incorrect or unrelated to the course outline, meaning they can be outdated and this can affect how students prepare for examinations.   
This platform will address this problem by providing one central place for students to access approved academic materials.

**4\. Existing Methods and Their limitations** 

Students in Babcock use different methods to get access to resources and academic materials.  
**4.1 Asking Senior Students**  
 Students tend to go to other higher-level students to get access to past questions and other materials.   
**Limitations: Students may not always know the right people to ask, and the materials they receive may not be complete.**   
**4.2 Google Drive**  
Some students also share past questions and other course materials to Google Drive.   
**Limitations: not every student has access to this Google Drive or shared files. These files or the resource materials can be scattered across the file itself, making it hard to trace the right past question or resource, making it hard to study.**   
**4.3 Searching Independently**  
Students tend to search online or look for materials on their own and this can be very stressful. At times they fail to do it.  
**Limitations:**  **this can take time and sometimes it may be incorrect, outdated, and may not be related to the course at all.**   
**4.4 Existing Academic Platforms**  
Students also find themselves using other academic platforms to find learning materials and they may not be in line with what they are learning in school currently.   
This is great but it comes with a lack of availability, low costs, and reliability of these resources and they may differ from each other. This proposed platform will focus on providing organized academic materials for only Babcock University students, completely for free.   
**5\. Proposed Solution**

Our solution is what we call a central digital library and examination platform for Babcock University.   
This platform will allow us, Babcock students, access to various academic resources based on our school or department, course code, academic level, and semester.   
Lecturers and authorized university staff will handle uploading and the management of learning materials. These materials will be checked against actual records to make sure that they are valid and they are up to date before students can access them.   
This platform will also support offline access. Students can download materials while connected to the internet and use them later without an internet connection. However when they reconnect, the platform will synchronize new or updated material and relevant saved practice results.   
Students will also be able to practice the past questions through an examination-style interface. This will include timers, automatic marking, scores, and feedback, just like an online exam would be.
